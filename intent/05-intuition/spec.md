## Structure

```markdown
# <Topic> — Intuition

*For: <audience> · Assumes: <prerequisites> · Covers: <scope>*
```

Use diagrams, examples, and concrete scenarios when they help the reader form
the model. Link to formal artifacts for normative details instead of embedding
requirements or spec clauses.
